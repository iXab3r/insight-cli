Native headless merge integration

HeadlessProgramMergeManager.java adapts orchestration from Ghidra 12.1.2
MergeManager.java, developed by the National Security Agency and distributed
under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (APACHE-2.0.txt). It is modified for
synchronous headless operation and refuses unresolved native decisions.
ProgramMultiUserMergeManager creates/orders all stock resolvers.

native-merge-patches.ts extracts six SHA-256-pinned upstream source files from
the selected Ghidra installation into owned compilation storage. The generated
sources retain their original license headers. Headless entry/display assumptions
are adapted. A separate native MemoryMergeManager compatibility correction covers
unchanged-null comments and symmetric deletion/update conflicts. The generated
native-merge-provenance.json records upstream and compiled source hashes.
Installed Ghidra JARs are not modified. No alternative object merger is provided.
